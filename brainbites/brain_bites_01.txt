Reading your notes again and again? 📚 Brain Bites #1

Going over the same page feels like studying, but your brain is mostly recognising it, not remembering it.

In a classic study, students either read a text again or tested themselves on it. After 5 minutes, reading again looked better. After ONE WEEK, self-testing won: 56% vs 42%.

Try it today:
1️⃣ Read once
2️⃣ Close the book and write down everything you remember
3️⃣ Check what you missed
